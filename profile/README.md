# Infinite Motion Xpress Inc.

Infinite Motion Xpress Inc. is a Philippine technology company building practical software for the way people move, work and access public services. Founded in Iloilo City, we design for the conditions our users actually face: patchy connectivity, shared devices, local regulations and tight budgets.

Our work spans two flagship products, **Xicar** for urban mobility and delivery, and **WorkForce360** for human resource management in local government units (LGUs) and private organizations.

## What we believe

- **Built for the Philippines first.** Local payment habits, statutory requirements and road realities shape our products from day one, not as an afterthought.
- **Reliable over flashy.** Offline-first apps, careful handling of money and data, and systems that keep running when the network does not.
- **Respect for the people who use our software.** Drivers, riders, employees and administrators all deserve tools that are fair, clear and fast.

## Xicar

Xicar is a mobility and delivery platform that connects customers with local drivers and merchants.

- **Xiclo** for quick two-wheel rides through traffic.
- **Xicar** for four-wheel rides when comfort and capacity matter.
- **Food and Mart** delivery from neighborhood restaurants and stores.

Xicar ships as three mobile apps, one each for customers, drivers and merchant attendants, backed by an operations console that lets our team manage fares, drivers, refunds and service quality in real time. Every peso that moves through the platform is tracked through a dedicated wallet service so riders, drivers and merchants are always paid correctly.

## WorkForce360

WorkForce360 is a human resource information system (HRIS) built for LGUs and private organizations of any size.

- **Attendance** with face liveness checks and GPS-verified clock-in from a mobile app that works offline.
- **Payroll** that handles Philippine statutory deductions, including BIR withholding tax, GSIS, SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG.
- **Leave and approvals** with configurable workflows that match an organization's own chain of command.
- **Directives and tasks** so urgent announcements are acknowledged and field work is documented with photo and location proof.

Each organization gets its own isolated tenant, deployed either on a dedicated on-site device or in our hosted cloud, so sensitive employee data stays under the organization's control.
